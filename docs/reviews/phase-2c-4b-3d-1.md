### High — The reveal contract claims a guarantee that its own implementation deliberately does not give

`revealReapplyReport` says that, when the report is above the scrollport, “the page moves by exactly the amount needed to show it,” and the decision record repeats that as a guarantee (`src/lib/components/reveal.ts:134`, `src/lib/components/reveal.ts:136`, `docs/decisions/2c-4b-3d-1-notes.md:178`, `docs/decisions/2c-4b-3d-1-notes.md:180`). The contract and record therefore need to make the movement guarantee conditional on a platform honoring the call; as written, they claim exactly the visual postcondition that the code and tests cannot force.

## Answers to the six questions

### 1. Truth of `differentMatch` and `gone`

Yes. `reresolve` reads only `view.matches[previous.position]`, returns `gone` when that entry is absent, and returns `differentMatch` only when the candidate's `source_text` fingerprint differs from the held fingerprint (`src/lib/browser/selection.ts:192`, `src/lib/browser/selection.ts:194`, `src/lib/browser/selection.ts:197`, `src/lib/browser/selection.ts:109`). The new `differentMatch` sentence says only that the text at the position differs and explicitly leaves open whether this is the same edited snippet or a different one (`src/lib/i18n/en.json:123`, `src/lib/i18n/es.json:123`). It therefore makes no unsupported identity verdict.

`gone` also has the second producer the record identifies: `repairSelection` maps `clearSelection` to `{ reason: 'gone', reloaded: null }`, while `identityRecovery` selects that action for `identityNoSuchMatch`, `identityWrongDocument`, and `unknownDocument` (`src/lib/browser/selection.ts:276`, `src/lib/browser/selection.ts:280`, `src/lib/browser/selection.ts:292`, `src/lib/ipc/errors.ts:745`, `src/lib/ipc/errors.ts:748`). Its new sentence is about the cleared selection no longer being pointable, explicitly disclaims removal, and says no file-wide search was made (`src/lib/i18n/en.json:124`, `src/lib/i18n/es.json:124`). That is true of both the out-of-range producer and the no-read producer, and it does not smuggle in an identity verdict.

Both keys are selected through the exhaustive typed `selectionNoticeKey` accessor rather than a constructed key (`src/lib/browser/notices.ts:133`, `src/lib/browser/notices.ts:137`, `src/lib/browser/notices.ts:139`), and the component renders the resulting notice through `tSelectionNotice` (`src/lib/i18n/index.ts:460`, `src/lib/components/DetailPane.svelte:637`).

### 2. `nearest`, its guarantees, and the controls below it

`nearest` is the right alignment choice for the measured geometry: the report was wholly above the visible band, while §11.4 forbids repairing it by scrolling farther down or changing the conflict panel's existing alignment (`docs/decisions/2c-4b-3c-2-window-reading.md:760`, `docs/decisions/2c-4b-3c-2-window-reading.md:768`, `docs/decisions/2c-4b-3c-2-window-reading.md:912`, `docs/decisions/2c-4b-3c-2-window-reading.md:915`). The DOM helper asks for `block: 'nearest'` and leaves `revealOutcome`'s `start`/`end` choices unchanged (`src/lib/components/reveal.ts:73`, `src/lib/components/reveal.ts:101`, `src/lib/components/reveal.ts:117`, `src/lib/components/reveal.ts:148`, `src/lib/components/reveal.ts:152`).

The documented non-guarantee about neighboring content is candid: revealing an above-the-fold report moves the outcome panel and its controls down, and the record says the required scroll distance grows by the report height (`src/lib/components/reveal.ts:138`, `src/lib/components/reveal.ts:140`, `docs/decisions/2c-4b-3d-1-notes.md:183`, `docs/decisions/2c-4b-3d-1-notes.md:187`). It also labels the predicted conflict-panel position as arithmetic rather than a measurement and hands that check to 3d-2 (`docs/decisions/2c-4b-3d-1-notes.md:188`, `docs/decisions/2c-4b-3d-1-notes.md:190`). The positive guarantee is not accurate, however, for the reason in the High finding: an absent or throwing platform produces no movement at all (`src/lib/components/reveal.ts:68`, `src/lib/components/reveal.ts:77`).

### 3. Fixing the sentence rather than splitting the predicate

The choice is substantively sound rather than merely easier. The collision predicate first rejects an unchanged intent, accepts an unchanged baseline state, then treats an unsatisfied `Remove` or `Set` as a collision (`src/lib/browser/matchEditor.ts:1853`, `src/lib/browser/matchEditor.ts:1857`, `src/lib/browser/matchEditor.ts:1861`, `src/lib/browser/matchEditor.ts:1864`). Baseline equality compares presence, value, and the eligibility verdict (`src/lib/browser/matchEditor.ts:1807`, `src/lib/browser/matchEditor.ts:1811`). Any collision blocks the same whole reapply and is returned as one `fieldCollisions` obstacle (`src/lib/browser/matchEditor.ts:2099`, `src/lib/browser/matchEditor.ts:2103`), while whole-target ineligibility is checked earlier and produces the separate `targetNotEditable` obstacle (`src/lib/browser/matchEditor.ts:2095`, `src/lib/browser/matchEditor.ts:2097`).

The widened sentence names the baseline-state categories and the refusal without claiming that two values conflict (`src/lib/i18n/en.json:255`, `src/lib/i18n/es.json:255`). The record's point that value and eligibility can differ simultaneously makes a split require either overlapping reasons or an extra presentation rule, without changing the recovery offered (`docs/decisions/2c-4b-3d-1-notes.md:260`, `docs/decisions/2c-4b-3d-1-notes.md:268`). Keeping the finding at Medium is consistent with the source record's distinction: the refusal and “nothing was written” result were correct, while the reason was false (`docs/decisions/2c-4b-3c-2-window-reading.md:935`, `docs/decisions/2c-4b-3c-2-window-reading.md:943`).

### 4. Spanish register and the count

The register fix is genuinely nine strings. Seven consecutive shared-family strings now use `tú` forms—`has conservado`, `pediste`, `envíalo`, `se te dirá`, `conservaste`, `Puedes`, and `tienes`—rather than `usted` forms (`src/lib/i18n/es.json:140`, `src/lib/i18n/es.json:146`). The editor's two strings likewise use `tu`/`tus`, including the two register-bearing cases the pronoun-only sweep would miss (`src/lib/i18n/es.json:255`, `src/lib/i18n/es.json:256`).

There is no tenth in the reapply family: `notAttempted` and the four shared obstacles are impersonal (`src/lib/i18n/es.json:147`, `src/lib/i18n/es.json:151`), as are the three creation obstacles, deletion obstacle, three move obstacles, and duplication obstacle (`src/lib/i18n/es.json:303`, `src/lib/i18n/es.json:305`, `src/lib/i18n/es.json:332`, `src/lib/i18n/es.json:374`, `src/lib/i18n/es.json:376`, `src/lib/i18n/es.json:410`). The separate inventory error in the Low finding is that the record later uses this register-only count as though it were the count for all localized values changed by the step (`docs/decisions/2c-4b-3d-1-notes.md:408`).

### 5. Falsifiability of the ten tests

All ten cases have assertions that can fail for the behavior each claims. The two model cases distinguish no report from every enumerated report outcome (`src/lib/browser/reapply.test.ts:402`, `src/lib/browser/reapply.test.ts:406`). The three DOM cases can respectively catch an unwanted call for `none`, a missing call or wrong target/alignment for `reportPanel`, and a null-element call or an uncaught platform refusal (`src/lib/components/reveal.test.ts:160`, `src/lib/components/reveal.test.ts:166`, `src/lib/components/reveal.test.ts:180`). The null/refusal case does not by itself prove the positive reveal, but the immediately preceding case does (`src/lib/components/reveal.test.ts:169`, `src/lib/components/reveal.test.ts:177`).

Each of the five mounted cases requires one `nearest` call to the bound report after the first refusal and another call after the second press, so removing a component's effect or deriving only the stable string cue makes its own case fail (`src/lib/components/MatchEditor.test.ts:1505`, `src/lib/components/MatchCreator.test.ts:1177`, `src/lib/components/MatchDeleter.test.ts:803`, `src/lib/components/MatchMover.test.ts:1093`, `src/lib/components/MatchDuplicator.test.ts:998`).
