## Findings

### High — `docs/decisions/2c-4c-5-window-reading.md:223-282, 564-585, 707-708, 786-790` — M2 is not latent, and the claimed harmlessness is not established

The central geometry verdict says that the zero-height `section.recovery` costs a person nothing in every state reached, that the host outcome after it was `null`, and that no launch constructed the sibling-overlap state. The source and the retained launches establish the opposite state history. P27-P34 all print `browser.reapply.manualResolution`, then an open recovery form. `attemptOfReapply` leaves the held session unchanged for `manualResolution` (`src/lib/browser/reapply.ts:540-547`), and that arm expressly leaves the conflict in place (`:228-239`). Both creating hosts render `<RecoveryPanel>` immediately before the host outcome conditional (`MatchEditor.svelte:895-912`; `MatchCreator.svelte:779-795`). Consequently, while each of P27-P34 had the form open, its host conflict outcome was still rendered as the following sibling. The record's statements that the reapply cleared the outcome, that `view.outcome` was `null`, and that no launch constructed the state are false.

The transcripts did not remeasure the host outcome after opening the form, so they do not establish the resulting rectangles or paint/hit-test result. They also cannot establish that the controls were clickable by a person: the driver uses `HTMLElement.click()`, which bypasses mouse hit testing, exactly as inherited §8.6 admits. A later-painted overlapping sibling could block a real pointer while every programmatic press still succeeds. The status panels ending at `y = 689` likewise do not prove that the pane's `scrollHeight` includes all overflow; no recovery launch prints the scroller's `scrollHeight` after the form opens.

M2 must therefore not be presented as a latent state inferred from a control roll or as inconsequential today. The retained launches constructed the prerequisite DOM state, but the instrument failed to measure the following sibling and cannot test pointer reachability. Reclassify the current evidence as an unresolved, potentially present overlap/hit-testing defect and retake it with both sibling rectangles, scroll extent, and a real hit-test or trusted pointer path before closing the geometry judgement.

## Verdict

**NOT READY.** The bilingual case coverage and final-byte comparisons are sound, and §3.1's layout-at-the-sampled-scroll-position conclusion is defensible. The record nevertheless repeats the project's worst defect class in several places: it converts post-images into first-attempt and build/gate chronology, final filesystem state into intermediate write history, and a reveal request plus a post-position into observed causation. Most importantly, M2's “latent and never constructed” premise is contradicted by the production state transitions and P27-P34: those launches opened the zero-height recovery form while the following host conflict outcome remained rendered, but did not measure that sibling or real pointer reachability. The geometry exit cannot be declared complete until the record is corrected and that constructed state is measured with an instrument capable of answering the overlap and hit-testing question.
