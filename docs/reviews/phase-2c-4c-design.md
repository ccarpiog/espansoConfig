# Phase 2c-4c — recovery fallback design consult

## VERDICT

2c-4c should add a recovery creator, reached only from an intact conflict, that keeps the original conflict and draft alive while it prepares one ordinary `create_match` against a person-chosen eligible destination, at that destination's end. For the match editor it carries the six supported scalar fields; for the creator it carries its two authored fields. It is deliberately labelled *Create a new snippet from supported fields*, not *Duplicate*, *Keep my draft*, or an unqualified byte-preserving copy. The trigger remains an explicit editable value, is never auto-renamed, and an exact repeated literal trigger interrupts the transaction with a new risk-only, candidate-content-addressed finding. The existing stacked draft/operation-versus-whole-file comparison and exact authored-text copy are the manual-resolution screen; no diff viewer is added. Delete, move, and duplicate recover by confirmed reload followed by a fresh selection and fresh operation, while raw recovery remains keep editing, exact copy, comparison, or confirmed reload. This needs a small Rust extension to `NewMatch` and the save findings, but no new command, no new writer, and no new patch operation.

## Q1 — Is save-as-new a create, and what is its trigger?

**Ruling: yes—on the match editor and creator, recovery is an ordinary `create_match` carrying an explicitly editable literal trigger, with a new transaction-produced finding when that literal trigger exactly repeats one already present in the destination.**

The honest product is the repository's already-named projection-based option: *New from supported fields*, with a disclosure of what was not copied; the split explicitly distinguishes that product from a true byte-exact duplicate (`docs/decisions/2c-split-notes.md:83-108`). The action label should therefore be **Create a new snippet from supported fields**. It must not say *Duplicate*, *exact copy*, or reuse *Keep my draft*: the latter already names reapplication of a draft to the newly parsed document (`docs/decisions/2c-split-notes.md:163-166`; `src/lib/browser/saveOutcome.ts:229-234`).

For a match-editor draft, “supported fields” means the six fields the model actually drafts: `trigger`, `replace`, `label`, `word`, `left_word`, and `right_word`; all sixteen other scalar fields and all four collections are currently sent as unchanged (`src/lib/browser/matchEditor.ts:991-1030`). The recovery value should derive each field's intended final state from baseline plus buffer through `fieldIntent`, because a buffer alone cannot distinguish an absent blank field from a present field cleared to blank (`src/lib/browser/matchEditor.ts:958-989`). A removed optional field is omitted; an absent optional field left blank is omitted; a retained or set eligible field is carried. `trigger` and `replace` remain mandatory. If either cannot be transferred as an editable non-empty string, recovery opens it blank with a localized explanation and requires the person to supply it before submission rather than inventing content. For a creator conflict, its draft already is exactly authored `trigger` and `replace` (`src/lib/browser/matchCreation.ts:215-230`).

This requires widening core `NewMatch` from two mandatory fields to those same two mandatory fields plus the four optional supported scalar fields. Today it is deliberately closed at `trigger` and `replace`, and `fields()` emits exactly those two in a fixed order (`crates/espansoconfig-core/src/draft/new_match.rs:13-28`, `:43-60`). The disclosure must say that comments, unknown keys, unsupported fields, key order, scalar spelling/quoting, tags, anchors, and other source syntax are not copied; the projection is read-only and cannot support a stronger promise (`CLAUDE.md:49-51`; `docs/decisions/2c-split-notes.md:89-105`).

The trigger control is seeded with the retained literal trigger only when that field has a transferable final value, and it stays editable. Do not append a suffix, guess uniqueness, or claim that a different spelling is semantically safe: D2u permits a risk claim and forbids an inferred scalar meaning (`CLAUDE.md:489-494`). When the candidate's newly inserted match has a modeled literal trigger text exactly equal to another modeled literal trigger text in that same destination sequence, `save_document` should return a new `FindingCode::NewMatchRepeatsLiteralTrigger { revision }`, classified `SuspiciousButPermitted`. Its localized explanation may say only that the new snippet repeats literal trigger text already present and that this application cannot determine how espanso will handle overlapping definitions. It must not say “invalid,” “collision,” which snippet wins, or that a non-match is safe.

That is the 2c-3c precedent transferred at the right level, not the existing code reused under a false name. `DuplicateKeepsTriggerDefinition` is produced only for `DuplicateItem` batches (`crates/espansoconfig-core/src/persist/save.rs:1477-1487`) and is bound to the exact candidate revision so consent for byte-different output cannot cross (`crates/espansoconfig-core/src/persist/save.rs:1504-1515`). The new finding should likewise be emitted only for an `InsertItem` candidate and content-addressed to the candidate. It may affect ordinary `create_match` too: exact repetition is a property of the candidate creation, not of which frontend route reached it. It must not become a generic validator rule that interrupts unrelated match edits.

## Q2 — Where does the recovered snippet go?

**Ruling: recovery defaults to the original document when it still has an eligible match sequence, otherwise requires an eligible destination choice, and always submits `NewMatchPosition::End` with no placement chooser.**

`NewMatchPosition` has three honest wire values—front, an identity-addressed after-anchor, and end—and an anchor is deliberately an identity rather than an ordinal (`src/lib/ipc/types.ts:2126-2149`). Recovery has no trustworthy old anchor by definition. `End` is the conservative fixed policy because it is semantic and is lowered afresh against the list that exists when the command runs; the existing reapply model already treats both front and end as anchor-free semantic choices (`src/lib/browser/matchCreation.ts:1517-1525`). Fixing one avoids making a recovery escape double as an ordering editor. A person who wants another position can perform a later same-sequence move as its own operation; a move remains same-sequence and alone in its batch under D2r/R25 (`CLAUDE.md:495-496`).

The destination and the placement are not silently inherited from the failed operation. The existing creator intentionally lists every file and attaches a typed refusal to ineligible ones rather than hiding them (`src/lib/browser/matchCreation.ts:36-47`). Recovery should use the same destination vocabulary, prefer the conflict's document only when the disk projection says it remains eligible, and require an explicit choice otherwise. It must show the destination path and the fixed “at end” placement before submission.

If the original `matches` sequence is gone, recovery must not synthesize one. `create_one_match` resolves the destination's match list before it constructs its one `InsertItem`, and a missing list is a command refusal (`src-tauri/src/commands.rs:1205-1220`, `:1243-1262`). Offer every other eligible destination. If none exists, write nothing, keep the original conflict and authored draft, keep exact copy available, and explain that no writable snippet list is available. That is an environmental lack of a destination, not permission to invent YAML structure or choose another file silently.

Opening the recovery creator must not first adopt the disk snapshot or close the source surface. The manual-resolution arm guarantees that the projection, selection, and one-shot authorization are untouched (`src/lib/browser/reapply.ts:228-239`). Preserve that advantage: build the recovery value from the conflict, and retain both it and the source conflict until `create_match` reaches a committed result. A refusal, another conflict, an adoption refusal, or a send whose outcome is uncertain must leave a recoverable value on screen; a committed create uses the ordinary post-save adoption and is never re-described as an error (`CLAUDE.md:413-419`).

## Q3 — What is manual resolution on screen?

**Ruling: manual resolution is the existing read-only comparison plus the truthful actions the draft kind supports; 2c-4c adds the recovery creator for authored match drafts, not a side-by-side diff tool or a copy for operation choices.**

*Compare* is already discharged in its v1 meaning. A conflict carries the exact whole-file disk text paired with the disk revision (`src/lib/browser/saveOutcome.ts:619-651`), and the match editor draws its retained fields followed by that whole file (`src/lib/components/MatchEditor.svelte:893-928`). The earlier consult deliberately chose this exact-text visual comparison over cross-revision match guessing or a textual diff (`docs/reviews/phase-2c-4a-design.md:74-85`), and the window reading found the stacked form legible (`docs/decisions/2c-4a-3c-2-window-reading.md:201-220`). 2c-4c should keep that comparison visible beside the `manualResolution` report. It should not add line alignment, a synthesized “same match,” a side-by-side requirement, or a diff result that feeds a write.

*Copy draft* is also existing behavior, not a new 2c-4c subsystem. `referenceCopyOf` preserves every authored field string inside a labelled, explicitly non-YAML reference (`src/lib/browser/saveOutcome.ts:1113-1141`), and `conflictChoicesFor` offers it only when the surface declares authored text (`src/lib/browser/saveOutcome.ts:454-469`). The clipboard implementation first tries the exact string API and refuses the textarea fallback for any text containing `\r`, rather than reporting a normalized copy as success (`src/lib/components/clipboard.ts:178-214`). Recovery must retain both the success and the honest failure message; it must not weaken that refusal.

The screen behavior by draft kind is:

- **Match editor:** retain the six-field draft, obstacle, and whole-file disk comparison; keep *Copy my text*; add *Create a new snippet from supported fields*. The recovery creator shows a transfer table—carried, omitted, or needs a value—plus destination and fixed end placement. It preserves the source conflict until the new creation commits.
- **Creator:** retain its two authored fields and comparison; keep copy; when automatic reapply failed because an `after` anchor or destination fact is unusable, offer the same recovery creator with the original disk document preferred and placement reset to end. The existing code already distinguishes an anchor refusal, a wrong destination, and an ordinary creation refusal as typed obstacles (`src/lib/browser/matchCreation.ts:1394-1441`).
- **Delete, move, duplicate:** show the existing retained-operation summary and whole-file disk text, then offer the existing confirmed reload. After adoption closes the old surface, the person reselects a live snippet and starts a new operation. These drafts are a positional choice or a revision-scoped identity, not authored content, so copying or “saving” them would preserve nothing while appearing to preserve something (`src/lib/browser/draftKind.ts:37-52`; `src/lib/browser/saveOutcome.ts:955-1009`).
- **Raw editor:** keep editing, exact copy, whole-document comparison, and confirmed reload are the complete v1 manual path. There is no match-shaped value to send to `create_match`, and the current model correctly says whole-document reapply would be either stale overwrite or forbidden merge (`src/lib/browser/rawEditor.ts:927-960`).

The new actions belong in a recovery choice producer keyed by `ReapplyOutcome.manualResolution`, not as ad hoc buttons appended by six renderers. `conflictChoicesFor` is already the sole authority for the base conflict choices (`src/lib/browser/saveOutcome.ts:412-469`); recovery should follow that model with its own discriminated choices, obstacle-to-action rule, typed i18n accessors, and English/Spanish strings. *Keep my draft* remains solely the reapply control.

## Q4 — Which surfaces get recovery?

**Ruling: all six write surfaces are in the recovery contract, but only the match editor and creator gain save-as-new behavior; operation-choice surfaces recover by fresh operation after reload, and raw recovers only as whole-document authored text.**

The five match surfaces can all produce `manualResolution`, and that arm carries each surface's obstacle while explicitly retaining the pre-adoption window (`src/lib/browser/reapply.ts:200-239`). They therefore all need a visible, obstacle-specific next step. Treating “has recovery” as synonymous with “can create a snippet” would immediately lie about deletion, movement, and true duplication.

The match editor and creator receive the recovery creator because their retained values are authored strings (`src/lib/browser/draftKind.ts:39-46`). The deleter and duplicator carry `MatchId`, while the mover carries a positional `MovePlacement`; their only honest recovery is to adopt the disk version, select from the live projection, and make a fresh request. In particular, a missing duplication target cannot be reconstructed as a true duplicate: `DuplicateItem` means copying the identified source item's exact owned runs, including comments and original line endings (`crates/espansoconfig-core/src/patch/edit.rs:823-847`). A projection-based new snippet is available only as the separately named recovery product, never as completion of the old duplicate operation.

Raw is **in** recovery and **out** of save-as-new. Its draft is exact whole-document text and its reload reseeds the draft, whereas all five match reloads close their surface (`src/lib/browser/saveOutcome.ts:246-265`). There is no document-creation command and no match to insert. Do not route raw text through `create_match`, do not add “save as another file,” and do not retry `ReplaceText`; the plan forbids automatic YAML merge and stale overwrite (`IMPLEMENTATION_PLAN.md:534-542`). Its existing comparison/copy/keep-editing/reload path is the recovery behavior 2c-4c must retain and include in the final evidence.

The checkable exit criterion against the phase's dead-end failure mode is: for every reachable `manualResolution` obstacle, the screen keeps the retained value and exact disk observation visible, names the obstacle, and exposes at least one truthful next action without an implicit write or discard. When an eligible destination exists, an authored match draft can reach a recovery creator and complete the ordinary refusal/acknowledgement/save protocol. An operation choice can reach a confirmed disk adoption and then a fresh operation. Raw can remain editable, copy, compare, or reload. If the environment supplies no writable match list or an exact clipboard route fails, the screen must say so and retain the draft; it must not manufacture a success. The starting guarantee that nothing was adopted or spent makes this criterion testable (`src/lib/browser/reapply.ts:228-239`).

## Q5 — Does this phase need Rust?

**Ruling: yes, but only to widen the existing creation value and add an authoritative repeated-trigger finding; all mutation still composes through `create_match` → `InsertItem` → `run_one_save` → `save_document`.**

The core change is `NewMatch` in `crates/espansoconfig-core/src/draft/new_match.rs`: retain mandatory `trigger` and `replace`, add optional `label`, `word`, `left_word`, and `right_word`, and make `fields()` emit only present schema-known fields in one documented order. No projection, comment, arbitrary key/value list, or YAML source enters this type. `create_one_match` already lowers `NewMatch::fields()` into exactly one `DocumentEdit::InsertItem` and routes it through `run_one_save` (`src-tauri/src/commands.rs:1205-1262`). No new `DocumentEdit` variant and no thirteenth Tauri command are justified.

The second core change is `FindingCode::NewMatchRepeatsLiteralTrigger { revision }` in `crates/espansoconfig-core/src/validate/mod.rs`, produced by a pure candidate inspection beside `findings_of` in `crates/espansoconfig-core/src/persist/save.rs`. It fires only for the newly inserted candidate when another match in that destination sequence exposes the same modeled literal trigger text. It is `SuspiciousButPermitted`, names risk only, and includes the exact candidate `ContentRevision`; the acknowledgement remains the transaction's exact-multiset round trip. The current enum documents that transaction-only findings live here precisely because they must participate in that protocol (`crates/espansoconfig-core/src/validate/mod.rs:206-220`), and the existing duplicate finding establishes the content-addressed pattern (`crates/espansoconfig-core/src/validate/mod.rs:370-395`).

Everything else is browser composition: retain the source `ConflictModel`, derive a recovery `NewMatch`, offer existing destination identities, submit fixed `{ End: {} }`, and send through `BrowserState.createMatch`. The six writers already share one `run_one_save` (`src-tauri/src/commands.rs:20-39`), and `save_document` remains the only entry point that may write, with no force flag and exact-multiset acknowledgement (`CLAUDE.md:399-404`). The core remains Tauri-free (`CLAUDE.md:43-51`).

## Step cut

1. **2c-4c-1 — creation and risk contract in Rust.** Widen `NewMatch`, add `NewMatchRepeatsLiteralTrigger`, and keep `create_match` lowering to one `InsertItem`. Rust model/persistence tests owe: optional-field order and omission; exact repeated and non-repeated literal triggers; no semantic claim for regex/unmodelled forms; candidate-revision changes invalidating old acknowledgement; exact-multiset coverage; and byte identity outside the insertion span. No frontend control changes. This isolates the only authoritative protocol change, following the precedent that transaction findings are produced inside the one save path (`crates/espansoconfig-core/src/persist/save.rs:1462-1488`).

2. **2c-4c-2 — recovery as browser values.** Add a shared recovery outcome/choice model, transfer decisions for all six match-editor fields, destination selection, fixed-end placement, and the rule that the source conflict survives until a recovery create commits. Compose `BrowserState.createMatch`; do not draw controls yet. Model/workspace tests owe every `manualResolution` obstacle from the five surfaces, no eligible destination, another conflict, refusal/acknowledgement/retry, uncertain send, failed adoption after a known commit, selection races, and proof that no command is called by operation-choice or raw recovery. Building the transition before offering it follows the existing capability pattern (`CLAUDE.md:221-229`).

3. **2c-4c-3 — one recovery UI, i18n, and mounted evidence.** Add the one recovery-choice producer, transfer disclosure, destination control, trigger editing, repeated-trigger acknowledgement presentation, and obstacle-specific guidance in English and Spanish through typed accessors. Change only the components that must draw those values; every changed component gets a mounted interaction test. The mounted matrix must prove the match editor and creator invoke recovery creation, delete/move/duplicate never offer copy or save-as-new, raw never offers save-as-new, and the original conflict remains after every non-committed ending. A component must call an accessor rather than construct a key (`CLAUDE.md:465-471`).

4. **2c-4c-4 — rebuild and review the window instrument.** Reconstruct the harness from `docs/decisions/2c-4b-3d-2a-instrument-rebuild.md`, add recovery plans and expected-byte fixtures, and prove it reaches all six surfaces before scheduling a reading. This step judges the instrument, not the screen. The repository explicitly records that the harness is gone and budgets rebuilding and reading as separate worker contexts (`PROGRESS.md:6682-6689`, `:6755-6763`).

5. **2c-4c-5 — bilingual window reading.** Read every surface's manual path, keyboard/focus/scroll reachability, recovery creation at end, changed destination, missing sequence, exact repeated-trigger acknowledgement, another conflict, copy success/failure disclosure, and committed-result adoption; byte-compare the entire configuration tree after every case. This is the phase's manual-window evidence, distinct from model and mounted tests (`CLAUDE.md:147-155`). Include at least one actual committed awkward corpus fixture with CRLF or BOM and one item-owned-comment/block-scalar case, verifying that pre-existing bytes survive and that the recovery disclosure does not call the synthesized item a duplicate. The broad fifteen-fixture sweep and the owner's real configuration remain outside this focused phase; the repository records that neither has ever gone through this harness (`PROGRESS.md:6783-6789`).

6. **2c-4c-6 — remove the instrument and restore production baselines.** Remove probe sources, hook lines, launch trees, and manifests; perform the residue sweep; then re-derive the harness-free gate counts rather than copying figures observed with the harness. This step adds no product evidence, but prevents an instrument from becoming production code; the previous removal record shows the two-part harness and the required distinction between with-harness and production counts (`docs/decisions/2c-4b-3d-3-notes.md:18-29`, `:106-139`).

## What this phase must not do

- Do not call the recovery product *Duplicate*, *exact copy*, or *Keep my draft*. It reconstructs schema-supported fields and therefore cannot promise source-byte preservation; *Keep my draft* is already the reapply control (`docs/decisions/2c-split-notes.md:89-105`; `src/lib/browser/saveOutcome.ts:229-234`).
- Do not auto-suffix, normalize, or silently replace the trigger. The person chooses the literal text; the application reports exact repeated-text risk and makes no espanso-semantic claim (D2u: `CLAUDE.md:489-494`).
- Do not make repeated-trigger detection a UI-only checkbox, preflight, or generic validator rule. The finding must come from the candidate inside `save_document`, be content-addressed, and affect creations only; otherwise consent is bypassable or unrelated edits are interrupted (`crates/espansoconfig-core/src/persist/save.rs:1504-1515`; `CLAUDE.md:399-404`).
- Do not add a force flag, a second writer, direct file replacement, or a command that copies `run_one_save`. All six existing writers already converge there, and bypassing `save_document` loses the lock, revision check, reparse, validation, acknowledgement, and backup (`src-tauri/src/commands.rs:20-39`; `CLAUDE.md:399-410`).
- Do not adopt the conflict snapshot merely to open recovery, and do not discard the source conflict before a committed recovery creation. The entry arm explicitly promises that adoption and authorization spending have not happened (`src/lib/browser/reapply.ts:228-239`).
- Do not guess an old anchor, reuse an old `MatchId`, choose a numeric position, or offer `After` in recovery. Wire anchors are revision-scoped identities and recovery has none; use fixed `End` (`src/lib/ipc/types.ts:2126-2149`).
- Do not create a missing `matches` sequence, choose another file silently, or broaden the operation into file creation. The existing command requires the match list before constructing its insertion (`src-tauri/src/commands.rs:1205-1220`).
- Do not add a textual/YAML merge, a diff whose result is writable, a reconstructed “same match,” or a stale raw `ReplaceText` retry. V1 explicitly forbids automatic three-way YAML merging and stale overwrite (`IMPLEMENTATION_PLAN.md:534-542`).
- Do not copy or save `MovePlacement`/`MatchId` operation drafts. They preserve no authored content, and the existing value-level distinction already refuses that false promise (`src/lib/browser/draftKind.ts:37-52`).
- Do not combine recovery creation with a move or any second edit. A later reorder is a separate same-sequence move, and R25 keeps a move alone (`CLAUDE.md:495-496`).
- Do not claim the clipboard succeeded after normalization or tell a person a rendered carriage-return name is their original text. The shared clipboard route deliberately refuses that case (`src/lib/components/clipboard.ts:184-201`).
- Do not hardcode any new user-facing sentence or construct i18n keys in a component. English and Spanish are mandatory, and typed accessors are the repository's compile-time coverage (`CLAUDE.md:38-41`, `:465-471`).
- Do not turn this phase into an exhaustive corpus or real-configuration survey. Its reading should close the fixture shapes directly relevant to recovery, while the full fifteen-fixture and real-use gap remains explicitly recorded for a later gate (`PROGRESS.md:6783-6789`).
